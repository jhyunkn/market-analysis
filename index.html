What this is based on

Every number on this tab comes from five inputs. None of them is a forecast; each has a known weakness, listed under it.

  • PricesDaily closes from Yahoo Finance since 2017, adjusted for splits and dividends, through the latest completed market close.This window is one long bull market with a few sharp breaks, so "normal" here reflects a generally favorable era.
  • Likely and possible rangesEach stock's own volatility: today's level (exponentially weighted, recent days count most) fading toward its 1-year average with a 3-month half-life. Bands are 2-in-3 and 9-in-10 outcomes with no up or down drift.Real moves have fatter tails than these bands; a crash can land outside the light band.
  • If the S&P repeats historyThe median S&P 500 path over the year after past sell-offs like today (VIX 30 or higher with the S&P 8–20% below its high, since 1990, each episode counted once), scaled by the stock's trailing five-year beta to SPY. Newer listings use all available post-IPO history.A median across few episodes; any single year can be far from it, and beta drifts.
  • Setups like this, for each stockPast days after the first one-year warm-up when the stock sat within 5 points of today's distance below its 52-week high and on the same side of its 200-day average. It shows how often it was higher a year later, against how often it was higher in any year, and counts episodes, not days. It counts only with 4 or more episodes.Small samples, and overlapping days inside one episode are not independent. Survivorship: these are stocks that are big today.
  • Analyst targetsRobinhood's consensus: buy/hold/sell counts and the low, average and high 12-month target, with the date it last changed when available.Targets lag prices; many were set before this sell-off and tend to be optimistic.
  • Action state and levelsFour checks score the stock: trend (price vs 200-day, 50-day slope), setups-like-this edge of 10+ points over its normal year, the S&P history path lifting it 5%+, and a reachable analyst target with 70%+ buy ratings. "Confirms above" is the nearer moving average above the price (or the 1-month high when above both). "Invalidates below" is the 3-month low, or one normal month down if closer.Rules written in advance, not a backtested strategy. Not advice.
